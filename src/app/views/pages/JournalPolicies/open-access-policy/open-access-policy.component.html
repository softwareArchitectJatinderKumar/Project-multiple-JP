Open Access Policy

Our journal is committed to providing unrestricted access to high-quality research and promoting the principles of open science. Our open access policy is designed to ensure that knowledge is freely accessible, allowing researchers, scholars, and the general public to benefit from the latest advancements in their respective fields. The following key components define our open access policy:

Open Access Model: Our journal follows the gold open access model, where all published articles are freely accessible to readers without any subscription or payment barriers.

Copyright and Licensing: Authors retain the copyright to their work and grant the journal a license to publish the article under a Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY). This allows others to distribute, remix, adapt, and build upon the work, provided appropriate credit is given to the original author.

Article Processing Charges (APCs): There is no article processing charge (APC) for the submission or Publication of paper in our journal. Our journal is committed to providing free and unrestricted access to high-quality research without any financial barriers. As part of our open access policy, we do not charge authors any Article Processing Charges (APCs) for the publication of their manuscripts. Our goal is to promote the widest dissemination of knowledge and to ensure that researchers and readers from around the world can access and benefit from the research published in our journal without any financial constraints. We believe that this approach enhances the accessibility and impact of scholarly work and encourages collaboration and engagement within the academic comunity.

Accessibility and Archiving: Our journal ensures the long-term accessibility of published articles through archiving in reputable digital repositories. We collaborate with established platforms to preserve and disseminate research for future generations.

Immediate Publication: Accepted manuscripts are promptly published online after undergoing peer review and editorial processes. This facilitates rapid dissemination of research findings.

Open Data and Supplementary Materials: We encourage authors to provide open access to their research data whenever possible. Supplementary materials associated with the article are also freely accessible.

Transparency and Attribution: We emphasize the importance of transparent reporting and attribution. Authors are encouraged to provide clear references, citations, and acknowledgments to ensure proper credit is given.

Indexing and Discovery: Our open access articles are indexed and discoverable through reputable databases, search engines, and scholarly platforms, enhancing their visibility and impact.

>Engagement and Collaboration: Open access fosters global collaboration and knowledge exchane. We encourage authors to engage with readers, share their work on social media, and participate in discussions to amplify the impact of their research.

Ethics and Integrity: Open access content is subject to the same rigorous peer review and ethical standards as subscription-based content. We uphold the principles of research integrity, transparency, and responsible conduct of research.

By adhering to this open access policy, our journal aims to contribute to the advancement of science, support the democratization of knowledge, and facilitate meaningful interactions among researchers, practitioners, policymakers, and the public.
