Conflict of Interest Policy

At Lovely Professional University, we are committed to upholding the highest standards of ethical conduct and transparency in all aspects of scholarly publishing. Our conflict of interest policy is designed to ensure the integrity and credibility of the research we publish. This policy outlines the principles and guidelines that authors, reviewers, editors, and other stakeholders must adhere to in order to avoid and manage potential conflicts of interest.

Definition of Conflict of Interest: A conflict of interest arises when an individual's professional, personal, or financial interests could compromise their objectivity, judgment, or decision-making in relation to the journal's processes.

Authors: Authors are required to disclose any potential conflicts of interest that could influence the interpretation or perception of their work. This includes financial interests, affiliations, sponsorships, or personal relationships that may have a direct r indirect impact on the research.

Reviewers: Reviewers are expected to declare any conflicts of interest that could affect their impartial evaluation of manuscripts. If a reviewer has a substantial conflict of interest, they will be recused from reviewing the manuscript in question.

Editors and Editorial Board: Editors and members of the editorial board are responsible for ensuring the fair and unbiased evaluation of submitted manuscripts. They must disclose any conflicts of interest related to specific submissions and avoid making editorial decisions in cases where their own interests could compromise the integrity of the review process.

Disclosure and Transparency: All conflicts of interest must be disclosed at the earliest possible stage in the submission, review, and editorial process. Authors, reviewers, and editors should provide complete and accurate information to enable the appropriate management of conflicts.

Management of Conflicts of Interest:

  1. Authors with conflicts of interest will be required to provide a clear and detailed statementdescribing the nature of the conflict in their manuscript.
  2. Reviewers and editors with conflicts of interest will be recused from the review process or editorial decisions pertaining to the relevant manuscript.
  3. Decisions regarding manuscripts involving conflicts of interest will be made by impartial individuals who are not affected by the conflict.

Transparency and Reporting: Our Journal will provide a statement of conflicts of interest in published articles. The journal may also periodically publish aggregated data on the occurrence and management of conflicts of interest to enhance transparency.

Training and Education: The editorial team, authors, and reviewers will be encouraged to undergo training and education on conflict of interest awareness and management.

Enforcement: Failure to adhere to this conflict of interest policy may result in corrective actions, including but not limited to manuscript rejection, suspension of editorial roles, and restriction of future submissions.

Continuous Review: This conflict of interest policy will be periodically reviewed and updated to ensure its effectiveness and alignment with best practices in scholarly publishing.

By adhering to this conflict of interest policy, [Your Journal Name] aims to maintain the highest ethical standards, protect the integrity of the research process, and ensure the trust of authors, reviewers, readers, and the broader academic community.
