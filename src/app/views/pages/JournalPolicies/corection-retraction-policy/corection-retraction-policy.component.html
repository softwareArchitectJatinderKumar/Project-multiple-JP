Corrections, Retraction, Withdrawal & Authorship Policy

Corrections: we are committed to maintaining the accuracy and integrity of the scholarly record. Corrections to published articles will be considered when errors, inaccuracies, or omissions are identified. The following principles guide our corrections policy:

  1. Corrections will be issued promptly upon the discovery of significant errors that impact the article's content, conclusions, or interpretations.
  2. Corrections will clearly identify the error, provide accurate information, and link to the original article.
  3. Corrections will undergo editorial review and approval before being published.

>Retraction: Retraction is a serious step taken when an article is found to be seriously flawed, misleading, or contains unethical content. Our retraction policy ensures transparency and accountability in such cases:

  1. Retractions are issued in cases of scientific misconduct, substantial errors, unethical research practices, or misleading data.
  2. A detailed retraction notice will be published that clearly states the reasons for the retraction and provides an explanation to readers.
  3. Authors will be given an opportunity to respond to allegations before a retraction decision is made.

Withdrawal: Authors may request the withdrawal of a submitted manuscript before publication or a published article under certain circumstances. Our withdrawal policy aims to facilitate this process while ensuring transparency:

  1. Manuscript withdrawal requests should be submitted in writing to the editorial office.
  2. Published article withdrawal requests will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis, considering the potential impact on the scholarly record.
  3. Withdrawn manuscripts or articles may still be accessible with a notice indicating the withdrawal.

Authorship Policy: Authorship is a fundamental aspect of scholarly publication, and we uphold principles that ensure proper credit and accountability. Our authorship policy emphasizes the following:

  1. All authors listed on a manuscript should have made significant contributions to the research and the article's content.
  2. Authorship order should reflect the extent of each author's contributions.
  3. The corresponding author is responsible for obtaining consent from all co-authors, verifying their contributions, and ensuring their agreement with the final version of the manuscript.
  4. Changes to authorship, including additions, deletions, or reordering, should be communicated to the editorial office before publication.

Enforcement and Dispute Resolution: Non-compliance with our Corrections, Retraction, Withdrawal, and Authorship Policy may lead to corrective actions, including corrections, retractions, or notifications to relevant institutions. In cases of disputes related to authorship, the editorial team will work to facilitate resolution and ensure fairness.

Continuous Review: This policy will be periodically reviewed and updated to ensure its effectiveness, alignment with best practices, and adherence to ethical standards in scholarly publishing.
