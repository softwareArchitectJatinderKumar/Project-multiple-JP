Peer Review Policy

The peer review policy for our journal outlines the procedures and principles governing theevaluation process of submitted manuscripts. Our journal is committed to upholding rigorous standards and ethical practices in peer review. The following key aspects define our peer review policy:

Objective Evaluation: Manuscripts will be reviewed objectively and impartially, with the aim of assessing their scientific quality, originality, and significance to the field.

Peer Review Models: Our journal employs a open peer review model. Reviewers' identities will be kept confidential according to the chosen model.

Selection of Reviewers: Reviewers will be selected based on their expertise in the subject matter of the manuscript. Conflicts of interest between authors and reviewers will be carefully managed.

Reviewer Guidelines: Reviewers will be provided with clear guidelines on evaluating manuscripts, including criteria for scientific validity, clarity, methodology, ethical considerations, and relevance to the journal's scope.

Review Timelines: The journal is committed to prompt review timelines. Reviewers are expected to submit their evaluations within [specified time frame] to ensure timely publication.

Confdentiality and Anonymity: Reviewers are required to maintain the confidentiality of the review process and the content of the manuscript. Authors' identities will be disclosed/kept confidential in accordance with the chosen peer review model.

Reviewer Feedback: Reviewers' feedback will be constructive, specific, and objective, aimed at helping authors improve the quality of their work. They will be encouraged to provide both strengths and areas for improvement.

Editorial Decision: The editor-in-chief or handling editor will make final decisions based on the reviewers' comments and the manuscript's quality. Authors will receive clear and transparent communication regarding editorial decisions.

Respecting Authorship and Originality: Reviewers will ensure that authors receive proper credit for their work, and that submitted manuscripts are original and do not contain plagiarized or duplicated content.

Ethical Considerations: Reviewers will be vigilant in identifying any ethical concerns, such as plagiarism, data fabrication, and conflicts of interest. Suspected breaches will be reported to the editorial team.

Reviewer Recognition: The journal acknowledges the valuable contributions of reviewers to the peer review process and may provide certificates or other forms of recognition.

By adhering to these peer review principles, our journal aims to ensure the credibility, validity, and excellence of the research we publish, while fostering a collaborative and respectful scholarly community.
