Plagiarism Policy

Journal is committed to maintaining the highest standards of academic integrity and expects all authors to uphold these standards. The journal has a zero-tolerance policy towards plagiarism and takes all allegations of plagiarism seriously.

Definition of Plagiarism: “Plagiarism is defined as the use of another person’s ideas, words, or work without proper attribution or permission”. This includes, but is not limited to, the following:

  • Using someone else’s work without citing the source
  • Paraphrasing someone else’s work without proper citation
  • Submitting a manuscript that has been previously published elsewhere
  • Submitting a manuscript that has substantial overlap with another published work,without proper attribution

Submission Requirements: Authors are required to submit original work that has not been published elsewhere. If the anuscript includes any previously published material or has been presented at a conference, the author must disclose this information at the time of submission.

Image and figures: Utilizing images, figures, and graphs in your work without acquiring appropriate permission and citing the source is an act of plagiarism. Therefore, authors are required to seek written permission from the owner of the content before using any image, figure, or graph in their work. Failure to obtain permission and provide proper citations can be considered plagiarism.

Detection Methods: All submissions to our journal are subject to a plagiarism check using software tools and/or manual review by the editorial team. Any instance of plagiarism will be reported to the author and may result in rejection of the manuscript.

Consequences: If plagiarism is detected, the manuscript will be rejected, and the author may be banned from submitting future work to the Journal. The author’s institution may also be notified of the plagiarism finding. In cases where plagiarism is suspected after publication, the article may be retracted, and the author may be banned from submitting future work to the Journal.

Use plagiarism detection software: Authors are encouraged to use plagiarism detection software to ensure that their work is original and properly cited.

Follow the journal’s submission guidelines: Authors should carefully follow the submission guidelines provided by the journal. This includes ensuring that the manuscript is properly formatted, contains all necessary sections, and includes proper citations.

Be transparent: Authors should be transparent about potential conflicts of interest, funding sources, and previous publications related to the work being submitted.

Review Process: The review process for manuscripts submitted to Journal includes a thorough plagiarism check. Any instance of plagiarism will be reported to the author and may result in rejection of the manuscript. If plagiarism is detected after publication, the article may be retracted, and the author may be banned from submitting future work to the journal.

Conclusion: The journal takes plagiarism very seriously and expects all authors to uphold the highest standards of academic integrity. By following the guidelines outlined in this policy, authors can help ensure that their work is original and properly cited.

By adhering to this open access policy, our journal aims to contribute to the advancement of science, support the democratization of knowledge, and facilitate meaningful interactions among researchers, practitioners, policymakers, and the public.
