Editorial Policy

The subsequent policies outline the instructions within our journal's publication procedure. The Journal embraces and endeavors to conform to the subsequent principles and prerequisites as provided by:

  • COPE – Committee on Publication Ethics
  • ICMJE – International Committee of Medical Journal Editors
  • OASPA - Open Access Scholarly Publishers Association

Editorial policies for journals outline the guiding principles and standards that the editorial team follows in managing the publication process and maintaining the quality and integrity of the journal's content. These policies help ensure consistency, transparency, and ethical conduct throughout the editorial process. While specific editorial policies can vary between journals, here are some common elements you might find in an editorial policy:

Scope and Focus: Clearly define the journal's scope, objectives, and the types of content it considers for publication. Specify the subject areas covered and the types of manuscripts the journal welcomes.

Editorial Independence: Emphasize the editorial team's autonomy and independence in making decisions regarding manuscript acceptance, rejection, and peer review. Highlight that editorial decisions are based on the quality and relevance of the research rather than external influences.

Peer Review Process: Describe the journal's peer review process, including the steps involved, the criteria for selecting reviewers, and the evaluation criteria for manuscripts. Clarify whether the journal uses single-blind, double-blind, open, or post publication peer review.

Ethical Guidelines: State the journal's commitment to ethical publishing practices. Address issues such as plagiarism, data fabrication, conflict of interest, and the responsible conduct of research. Explain how ethical violations will be handled.

Author Guidelines: Provide detailed instructions for authors on how to prepare and submit manuscripts. Specify formatting requirements, citaion style, word limits, and any additional materials that should be included with submissions.

Conflict of Interest: Outline how potential conflicts of interest for authors, reviewers, and editors will be identified and managed. Describe the steps that should be taken to disclose and handle conflicts.

Open Access and Copyright: Explain the journal's policies regarding open access publishing, including any associated fees. Detail the copyright and licensing terms that authors will need to adhere to upon acceptance.

Data Sharing and Transparency: Encourage authors to share research data and provide transparency in reporting methodologies and results. Specify any requirements for data availability and sharing.

Publication Ethics and Integrity: Highlight the journal's commitment to maintaining the integrity of the scholarly record and adhering to established publication ethics guidelines, such as those outlined by COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics).

Retractions and Corrections: Outline procedures for handling retractions, corrections, and expressions of concern in case of errors or misconduct.

Editorial Board and Reviewers: Provide information about the composition and roles of the editorial board and reviewers. Highlight their expertise and commitment to maintaining high editorial standards.

Appeals and Complaints: Explain the process for authors to appeal editorial decisions or lodge complaints about the review process.

Publication Schedule: Detail the journal's publication schedule, including expected timelines for review, acceptance, and publication.

Editorial Transparency: Commit to transparency in editorial decisions by providing authors with clear explanations for rejection and constructive feedback for improvement.

Post-Publication Policies: Address the journal's stance on post-publication discussions, corrections, and updates to published articles.
