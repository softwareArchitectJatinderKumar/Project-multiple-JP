Cross-Mark Policy

Our journal adheres to a cross-mark policy that encourages the responsible and ethical crossreferencing of content within and outside of our publication. This policy is designed to promote the proper citation and linking of relevant research while upholding the integrity of scholarly communication. The key principles of our cross-mark policy are as follows:

Citation and Attribution: Authors are encouraged to appropriately cite and attribute any content, ideas, or data that have been previously published in our journal or in other reputable sources. Proper citation ensures transparency, acknowledges intellectual contributions, and supports the scholarly record.

Cross-Referencing Within Our Journal: Authors are welcome to refer to and cite other articles published in our journal to build upon existing knowledge and create meaningful connections within our scholarly community. Cross-referencing should be done with accuracy and integrity.

External Cross-Referencing: Authors should responsibly cross-reference external sources, including content from other journals, books, websites, or databases. Proper attribution and accurate linking should be provided to guide readers to the original sources.

Avoiding Misrepresentation: Authors should avoid misrepresenting the context or content of cross-referenced materials. Cross-referencing should be done in a manner that enhances the reader's understanding and provides meaningful insights.

Preventing Plagiarism: Authors must ensure that cross-referencing is conducted in compliance with copyright and ethical guidelines. Direct copying of content without proper attribution constitutes plagiarism and is strictly prohibited.

Permissions and Licensing: Authors should obtain necessary permissions or licenses when cross-referencing copyrighted material beyond what is considered fair use. Authors are responsible for adhering to copyright and licensing terms.

Transparent Linking: Authors are encouraged to provide accurate and transparent links to cross-referenced content. Broken or misleading links hinder the reader's access to the referenced material and should be avoided.

Editorial Oversight: The editorial team ensures that cross-referencing within our journal adheres to our policies and guidelines. Cross-referenced content is subject to the same quality and ethical standards as original submissions.

Dispute Resolution: If disagreements or concerns arise regarding cross-referencing or citations, authors, readers, or stakeholders may communicate with the editorial team for resolution.

Our cross-mark policy reflects our commitment to fostering a collaborative and responsible academic environment. By following these principles, we aim to facilitate the exchange of knowledge, enhance research connections, and maintain the credibility and integrity of our journal and the broader scholarly community.
